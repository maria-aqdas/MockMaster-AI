# 🎯 MockMaster AI

An intelligent, interactive interview preparation workspace built with Streamlit. MockMaster AI transforms resumes into structured, personalized interview roadmaps—helping candidates uncover hidden skill gaps, structure behavioral answers, and practice real-time scenarios.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [MockMaster AI on Streamlit](https://mockmaster-ai-ihbmmbf9we5mr8xrwmdkez.streamlit.app/)

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Flexible Resume Input:** Upload files in PDF, DOCX, or TXT formats (up to 200MB) or paste raw resume text directly.
* **11-Stage Preparation Pipeline:**
  1. **Candidate Overview:** High-level summary of your background, industry alignment, and career positioning.
  2. **Core Skills:** Identification and mapping of domain-specific technical and soft competencies.
  3. **Missing Signals:** Detection of absent metrics, omitted responsibilities, or unclear project outcomes.
  4. **Strengths vs. Gaps:** Direct comparison of your competitive edges against typical industry expectations.
  5. **Interview Red Flags:** Highlights potential risks (e.g., unexplained tenure gaps, buzzword density) before interviewers spot them.
  6. **Question Bank:** Tailored behavioral, technical, and situational questions aligned with your background.
  7. **Top 5 Priorities:** High-yield preparation focal points for rapid turnaround before an upcoming round.
  8. **STAR Answer Blueprint:** Guided breakdown (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for structuring impactful stories.
  9. **Curveball Coach:** Practice unexpected, lateral-thinking, and situational scenario prompts.
  10. **Readiness & Advice:** Objective scorecard assessing preparedness, along with actionable tactical tips.
  11. **Mock Interview Simulator:** Dynamic, turn-based conversational coach to practice responding under interview pressure.
